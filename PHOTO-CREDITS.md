## Delivery and launch check
The first design pass references Pexels' image CDN with responsive widths and an explicit intrinsic aspect ratio. The original image was visually reviewed through the browsing tool. Downloading the image into the local build environment and checking transformed CDN variants were unavailable in this session; those variants must be verified in a network-enabled preview before merge. Prefer downloading licensed copies and serving optimized local AVIF/WebP/JPEG assets before production launch. Preserve this source/license record. Do not substitute another image without checking its license.
